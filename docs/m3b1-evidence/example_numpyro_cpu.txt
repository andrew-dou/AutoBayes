Linear predictor
  mu_i = a_c + (x_dose - mean(x_dose)) * b_dose + u[site_i] + x_dose,i * v[site_i]
  Predictors are centred at their sample means (center_predictors=True, as in
  brms and Bambi).
  a_c is the expected response at the predictor means (group effect zero); the
  prior on the intercept applies to a_c. The posterior reports the raw-scale
  intercept a = a_c - sum_p mean(x_p) * b_p, computed after sampling.

Group effects (non-centred)
  u[j] = sd_site * z0[j],  z0[j] ~ Normal(0, 1),  j = 0..23
  v[j] = sd_dose|site * z1[j],  z1[j] ~ Normal(0, 1),  j = 0..23
  u and v are independent by construction ('||'): separate SDs, independent offsets, no correlation
  parameter. v[j] multiplies the RAW x_dose (the group column is not centred, checked at build time),
  so the independence is stated for the intercept at x_dose = 0; shifting x_dose by c would make
  u' = u + c v correlated with v. brms's '(1 + x | g)' (correlated, LKJ prior) is a different model
  and is refused in this slice.
