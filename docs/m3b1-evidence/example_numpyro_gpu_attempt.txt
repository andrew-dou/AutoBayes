360 rows, 24 sites, dose varies within every site (min within-site SD 0.65)
the single-bar (correlated) form is refused: '(1 + dose | site)': '(1 + dose | site)' is brms's CORRELATED varying intercept and slope (an LKJ prior on their correlation), which is not supported in this slice

AutoBayes M0 model and an independent varying slope
  formula   : y ~ dose + (1 + dose || site)
  family    : gaussian, identity link
  data      : n = 360 observations, J = 24 levels of site
  levels    : 0=s00, 1=s01, 2=s02, 3=s03, 4=s04, 5=s05, 6=s06, 7=s07, 8=s08, 9=s09, 10=s10, 11=s11, 12=s12, 13=s13, 14=s14, 15=s15, 16=s16, 17=s17, 18=s18, 19=s19, 20=s20, 21=s21, 22=s22, 23=s23

Linear predictor
  mu_i = a_c + (x_dose - mean(x_dose)) * b_dose + u[site_i] + x_dose,i * v[site_i]
  Predictors are centred at their sample means (center_predictors=True, as in
  brms and Bambi).
  a_c is the expected response at the predictor means (group effect zero); the
  prior on the intercept applies to a_c. The posterior reports the raw-scale
  intercept a = a_c - sum_p mean(x_p) * b_p, computed after sampling.

Group effects (non-centred)
  u[j] = sd_site * z0[j],  z0[j] ~ Normal(0, 1),  j = 0..23
  v[j] = sd_dose|site * z1[j],  z1[j] ~ Normal(0, 1),  j = 0..23
  u and v are independent by construction ('||'): separate SDs, independent offsets, no correlation
  parameter. v[j] multiplies the RAW x_dose (the group column is not centred, checked at build time),
  so the independence is stated for the intercept at x_dose = 0; shifting x_dose by c would make
  u' = u + c v correlated with v. brms's '(1 + x | g)' (correlated, LKJ prior) is a different model
  and is refused in this slice.

Priors
  a_c ~ Normal(mu=0, sigma=5)
  b_dose ~ Normal(mu=0, sigma=2)
  sd_site ~ HalfNormal(sigma=1)
  sd_dose|site ~ HalfNormal(sigma=1)
  sigma ~ HalfNormal(sigma=1)

Likelihood
  y_i ~ Normal(mu_i, sigma)

PyMC variables sampled (value space)
  sigma_log__              log(sigma)
  Intercept                a_c
  dose                     b_dose
  1|site_sigma_log__       log(sd_site)
  1|site_offset            z0 (varying-intercept offsets)
  dose|site_sigma_log__    log(sd_dose|site)
  dose|site_offset         z1 (varying-slope offsets)
Deterministic: 1|site = u, dose|site = v, mu = linear predictor

group design check: Z_slope equals raw dose times the one-hot site matrix -> True
