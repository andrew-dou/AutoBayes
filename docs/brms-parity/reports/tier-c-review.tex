\noindent Spline phase~2 (\code{s()}) is not in this list: it waits for your licensing decision (Section~\ref{sec:decisions}).
Every agent wrote its feature from brms's documentation, Stan data and outputs, never from brms's, loo's or mgcv's
GPL source; C07's PSIS follows ArviZ's Apache-2.0 implementation and the published papers, with attribution in the
docstrings.

\paragraph{How close.} Measured with the gates' own comparisons on the chain's final tree: all 97 ungated cases pass,
with worst relative errors over every case of $8.4\times10^{-10}$ in the log density, $7.2\times10^{-9}$ in the gradient,
$5.2\times10^{-14}$ in predictions and $4.2\times10^{-15}$ between the JAX and PyTensor densities. The 25 cases new in
Tier C and P6 sit near $10^{-15}$, with three exceptions:
\begin{itemize}
\item the log density of mixtures under brms's default half-Student-$t$ priors (up to $5.8\times10^{-10}$), which is
  PyMC's float32 $\log 2$, the follow-up from Tier B;
\item the cloglog cases, where brms's own arithmetic is inexact: its log density by up to $1.9\times10^{-10}$, and its
  gradient by $6.3\times10^{-9}$ on \code{binomial\_cloglog}, all inside tolerance;
\item the exact Gaussian process, whose Cholesky factor of a matrix with condition number up to $10^{7}$ costs
  $8.9\times10^{-12}$ in the gradient and $5.2\times10^{-14}$ in predictions.
\end{itemize}
\noindent The three cases accepted against two references are reported in \#47: AutoBayes is within $3.7\times10^{-14}$ of
the 50-digit reference wherever brms is not.

\section{What combining the features found}
\label{sec:integration}

Because CI was down, the six unmerged features were rebased one onto the next and each was gated, and the whole
non-sampling suite run, on the combined tree. Every link passed: 2{,}255 tests after C01, 2{,}355 after C03, 2{,}498 after
C02, 2{,}609 after C04 and 2{,}748 after C06, with no failure at any step. Combining them was worth doing, because each
feature had been written against a \code{main} that lacked the others, and several pairs silently accepted something no
brms case verifies:

\begin{itemize}
\item \code{gp()} inside a multivariate formula built a model (a process per response); \code{gp()} next to \code{ar()} was
  built as well, and a third formula with \code{gp()} in an \code{mi()} model would have put a process inside it.
\item \code{ar}/\code{ma} priors at the top level of a multivariate, mixture or non-linear model, and \code{sdgp}/\code{lscale}
  on a multivariate model or a mixture, were accepted and ignored.
\item In a non-linear model, \code{me()} in a parameter's formula was accepted once priors were given, and \code{mi()} there
  crashed with an index error.
\end{itemize}

\noindent All of these are now refused with a message naming the construct, each with a test. The rule applied is the
loop's: a combination is offered only where a frozen brms case verifies it. Several of these refusals are stricter
than brms, which accepts, for example, \code{gp()} next to \code{ar()} and in multivariate formulas; they are listed in
the divergence rows.

\paragraph{A real Linux failure.} C07's first hosted run, before the billing block, failed 12 of 2{,}133 tests on Linux:
its loo probes regenerated their random inputs, and numpy's normal and Student-$t$ draws go through \code{exp}/\code{log},
whose last bit differs between macOS and Linux builds, so an exact hash of the inputs differed. The probes now check
the inputs rounded to 8 decimals, and I confirmed that all 30 still match loo's outputs with a third of every input
moved by one ulp. The fix has not yet run on Linux.

\section{How the claims were checked, and their limits}
\label{sec:verification}

\begin{itemize}
\item Each feature: the gate tool on the tree that will merge; the full non-sampling suite on the combined tree; the
  adapter and gate changes read for weakening (all extensions; C06 made two response checks stricter, since a NaN in a
  response had compared as equal); every earlier case model unchanged.
\item Reproducibility: \code{oracle/regen.py check --all} regenerated all 98 frozen fixtures from R on the chain's
  final tree and compared them with the committed files: all 98 identical.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Limits I know of.} The two new fitting paths (multivariate and non-linear models are built without Bambi)
are tested only with the sampler replaced, because nothing in the loop samples; the first real fit of either is
untested. C06 accepts a few combinations beyond its frozen cases, checked against hand-written \code{scipy} densities
rather than brms: several \code{mi()} terms, imputations that read other imputed columns, and \code{me()} beside other
terms. \code{binomial\_cloglog}'s seeded points were drawn from a narrower box after brms's own density was $-\infty$ at the
default one. The hosted sampling tests have not run on anything after C05.

\section{Process and cost}
\label{sec:cost}

Agent tokens reported: P6 0.63M, C05 0.80M, C07 0.68M, C01 0.75M, C03 0.75M, C02 0.91M, C04 0.70M, C06 0.88M, plus
1.59M for the three rebases handed back to their agents (C02 0.31M, C04 0.92M, C06 0.36M), about 7.7M in all, against my
estimate of 4.5--7M. Resuming an agent for its rebase was the expensive step: a resumed agent re-reads its whole
earlier context. I would do rebases myself in future, as I did for C07, C01 and C03. The machine's load reached 141
while three agents ran their suites at once; after that I ran nothing in parallel.

\section{Decisions}
\label{sec:decisions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{CI.} Either restore GitHub Actions (billing or spending limit), after which I merge \#49 and the five
  local features one at a time, each with hosted CI (about half an hour each); or tell me to merge on local
  verification alone, which means the full non-sampling suite and the oracle on each tree, without the sampling tests
  hosted CI runs.
\item \textbf{Splines.} AutoBayes is MIT; the spline module from phase~1 follows mgcv's (GPL) R functions step by step.
  My recommendation is a clean-room rewrite from Wood's published algorithms, keeping the same tests against mgcv's
  output; the alternatives are licensing the module or the package under the GPL, or dropping \code{s()}.
\item \textbf{Attribution.} C07's PSIS code follows ArviZ's (Apache-2.0) steps, with attribution in the docstrings. Do
  you want a formal \file{NOTICE} file as well?
\item \textbf{Cleanup.} Twenty-seven local worktrees now belong to merged or closed branches: the twenty-four
  listed in the Tier B review, plus \file{docs-tierB}, \file{parity-p6} and \file{parity-c05}. The six unmerged Tier C
  branches, \file{parity-b04} and the three permanent checkouts stay. I will remove the twenty-seven only on your yes.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Follow-ups found and not done.} PyMC's half-Cauchy and half-Student-$t$ priors add $\log 2$ in float32
($1.9\times10^{-9}$ per prior); mixtures have no per-row log density yet, so \code{log\_lik()} and \code{loo()} are not
offered for them; \code{weights()} for the zero-inflated and hurdle families; the learned-$\nu$ \code{describe()} order
that depends on Python's hash seed; frozen brms cases for the combinations refused above, wherever you want them.
