# Known divergences from brms

Where AutoBayes knowingly differs from brms. A fixture is never edited to hide one of these; the entry says what
the oracle shows, why AutoBayes differs, and whether the brms behaviour is offered as an opt-in or parked.

| id | area | brms | AutoBayes | status |
|----|------|------|-----------|--------|
| D1 | priors | fills in default priors (`default_prior()`) for every class | requires every prior explicitly; a class the model lacks is refused; brms's defaults are available on request, `ab.default_priors(formula, data, ...)` returning them as a `Priors` to pass in, each marked as a default in `describe()` and fit records | kept, with the opt-in (A07, docs/default-priors.md): gate G6 checks `ab.default_priors()` against the `default_prior()` table of every fixture, and five fixtures frozen without priors check the densities under the defaults |
| D2 | priors | an intercept-only model needs no coefficient prior | no `b` prior is needed either; one given anyway is accepted and changes nothing (the same target identity) | resolved (A07): `ab.default_priors()` returns no `b` prior for an intercept-only formula, as brms's table has no `b` row |
| D3 | factor levels | levels in R factor order (locale-dependent unless declared) | levels sorted as strings | handled: the oracle matches levels by content; cases declare levels in C-locale order |
| D4 | binomial | keeps rows with zero trials (they contribute no likelihood) | drops zero-trial rows before building the design and reports them | kept; cases avoid zero-trial rows |
| D5 | binomial predictions | `posterior_epred` is `trials * p` | `kind="mean"` returns `p` and `expected = trials * p`; the oracle compares `expected` | documented |
| D6 | new group levels | `sample_new_levels = "uncertainty"` by default | refuses by default (`new_groups="error"`); `resample_observed` and `population` are explicit | documented; stochastic, outside the gates |
| D7 | Bernoulli likelihood | `bernoulli_logit_lpmf` | the same density written with a gradient-stable softplus (not Bambi's floored form) | equal to 1e-8 relative on the fixtures |
| D8 | varying slopes | `y ~ (1 + x \|\| g)` adds a group-level slope on `x` without a population-level `x` | a varying slope must also be a numeric population-level term of the formula; `y ~ (1 + x \|\| g)` is refused with the form to write | kept (A01): the population-level slope is the slope's centre, and omitting it is a different model |
| D9 | Gamma link | `brmsfamily("Gamma")` defaults to the `inverse` link, `mu = 1 / eta` (`log` and `identity` on request) | `ab.gamma` has the `log` link only; the fixture `gamma_varint` was frozen with `link = "log"`, so the oracle compares like with like | kept (A09): `log` keeps `mu > 0` for every `eta` and puts the coefficients on the Poisson/negative binomial scale; the inverse link is not offered |
| D10 | offset | `offset()` takes any expression (`offset(log(x) + 1)`, `offset(2 * x)`) and several offset terms are summed | only `offset(col)` and `offset(log(col))`, one per formula; anything else is refused with the column to compute (in-formula transforms are A05) | kept (A06): every accepted form is checked against the data and against Bambi's evaluation; the fixture `poisson_offset` uses the accepted form |
| D11 | grouping syntax | `g:h:k`, `g/h/k` and `g/h:k` combine three or more grouping columns | only two columns can be combined in a grouping factor; the three-way and mixed forms are refused naming the construct | kept (A03): not in this slice; the fixtures `gauss_nested_colon` and `gauss_nested_slash` are two-column |
| D12 | grouping syntax | `(1 \| g:h) + (1 \| h:g)` builds two groups over the same partition of the rows; two combinations whose `_`-joined labels coincide (`a_b` with `c`, `a` with `b_c`) merge into one level | both refused: the same partition written twice, and colliding labels, naming the terms or the combinations involved | kept (A03): the first is a non-identified duplication, the second a silent merge |
| D13 | Beta likelihood | `beta_lpdf(Y \| mu * phi, (1 - mu) * phi)` with `mu = inv_logit(eta)` and `1 - mu` formed by subtraction, which is exactly zero beyond `eta` of about 37 | the same density through PyMC's `Beta(alpha, beta)` with `1 - mu` computed as `expit(-eta)`, positive for `\|eta\|` up to about 745; Bambi's `beta` family (`mu`, `kappa`) is not used so that the graph keeps the linear predictor `logit_mu` and brms's name `phi` | equal to 1e-8 relative on the fixture `beta_varint` (A11); the two differ only in rounding, and only where brms's form has already lost `1 - mu` |
| D14 | interactions | `y ~ x:w`, `y ~ z:h`, `y ~ x:w:z` are accepted: a missing main effect changes the coding of the interaction (R's marginality rule), and interactions have any order | every two-way interaction needs both main effects in the formula (`x + w + x:w` or `x*w`); three-way and higher interactions are refused naming the term | kept (A04, extending M3a-2's rule): omitting a main effect is a different model built under a different coding; the fixtures `gauss_interaction_numeric_numeric` and `gauss_interaction_categorical_categorical` have both main effects |
| D15 | interactions | any term can be an operand of an interaction, including `offset()` and transformed terms (`log(x):z`) | an interaction operand must be a main-effect column of the formula; `offset()` and transformed terms are refused as operands | kept (A04, A05): the offset has no coefficient to interact; an interaction with a transformed term is not in this slice (compute the column and interact it) |
| D16 | transforms | any R expression is evaluated into the design (`scale(x)`, `poly(x, 2)`, `log(x + 1)`, `I(x * w)`; `sqrt(0)` is `0`) | only `log(col)`, `exp(col)`, `sqrt(col)`, `I(col^k)` (integer `k`), `I(col * k)`, `I(col + k)`, `I(col - k)` of one numeric column; anything else is refused with the supported list; `log()` and `sqrt()` need positive values on every row and a non-finite result is refused | kept (A05): every accepted form is evaluated by the library and checked against Bambi's column; the fixture `gauss_transforms` uses `log(xpos)` and `I(x^2)` |
| D17 | probit likelihood | `bernoulli_lpmf(Y \| Phi(eta))` and `binomial_lpmf(Y \| trials, Phi(eta))`, with Stan's `Phi` exactly 0 below `eta = -37.5` and exactly 1 above `8.25`, so the density is `-inf` and the gradient NaN there | the same density through a stable `log Phi` (`erfc` / `log1p` for `eta >= -1`, `log(erfc / 2)` to `-20`, the Mills-ratio series below), finite with a finite gradient for every finite `eta` on both paths; the graph keeps `eta` as `probit_p` | equal to 1e-8 relative on the fixtures `bernoulli_probit` and `binomial_probit` (A12); the two differ only where brms's form is already `-inf` |
| D18 | cloglog link | `bernoulli(link = "cloglog")` and `binomial(link = "cloglog")`: `bernoulli_lpmf(Y \| inv_cloglog(eta))`, whose gradient for a `y = 0` row forms `1 - theta` by subtraction and loses `eps * exp(exp(eta))` relative (1e-7 at `eta = 3`; `-inf` above about 3.7) | `link="cloglog"` is offered (P6): `log P(y = 1) = log1mexp(-exp(eta))` and `log P(y = 0) = -exp(eta)` (`likelihoods.py`), finite with a finite gradient for every finite `eta` on both paths and tested against a 50-digit reference on `\|eta\| <= 40`; the graph keeps `eta` as `cloglog_p` | accepted against two references (P6; parked in A12): the frozen fixture `bernoulli_cloglog` records at point 5 (0-based: the sixth of the eight seeded points) a brms gradient of `-1.00559172179031` where the exact value is `-1.00559210649608` (3.8e-7 relative, `GRAD_REL` 1e-7; rows 29 and 34 have `y = 0` at `eta = 2.71` and `3.01`); the case opts in for its gradient, and 2 of its 144 gradient entries (`b[1]` at point 5, with and without the Jacobian) pass on the committed 50-digit reference, which AutoBayes matches to 7.3e-15 at every entry; everything else agrees with brms. `binomial_cloglog`, frozen with P6 on the box (-1, 1) of seeded points (at the default (-1.2, 1.2) brms's density is `-inf` at a seeded point, which is why A12 could not freeze it), needs no fallback: brms is within 6.3e-9 of the reference there |
| D19 | other links | `bernoulli` and `binomial` also take `cauchit`; `gaussian` and `student` take `log` and `inverse`, `poisson` and `negbinomial` `identity` and `sqrt`, `Gamma` `identity`, `Beta` `probit`, `cloglog` and `cauchit`, among others | only the logit and probit links of the Bernoulli and binomial families; `link=` is refused for every other family, naming the family's one link and the families that take `link=` | kept (A12): each further link needs its own stable likelihood form and a frozen fixture |
| D20 | correlated slopes | correlation IDs share one correlation matrix across group terms, `(1 + x \|p\| g) + (0 + w \|p\| g)`; `gr(g, cor = FALSE)` is another spelling of `\|\|`; the LKJ prior defaults to `lkj(1)` and `class = cor` without `group` applies to every correlated factor | one correlation matrix per `(1 + x \| g)` term over exactly its coefficients; `\|ID\|` and `gr()` are refused naming the construct (`\|\|` is the independent form); `cor={factor: ab.LKJ(eta)}` must be given for every correlated factor (D1; a single correlated factor also takes the bare prior) and is refused for an independent or intercept-only term | kept (A02): the per-term matrix is brms's own construction without IDs, verified against the fixture `gauss_slope_correlated`; correlations across terms are a different model |
| D21 | correlated slopes | each correlation is reported as a scalar per pair of coefficients, `cor_g__Intercept__x` (Stan's `cor_1`, the upper triangle of `Cor_1`) | the posterior carries the whole correlation matrix `cor\|g` (brms `Cor_1`) and its Cholesky factor `L\|g` (brms `L_1`, sampled on Stan's `cholesky_corr` coordinates `L\|g_cholesky_corr__`), both with dims `g__cor_dim` x `g__cor_dim_T` labelled by coefficient; brms's `cor_g__Intercept__x` is `cor\|g` at (`Intercept`, `x`) | documented (A02): names and layout only; the density with and without the Jacobian and every gradient coordinate agree to 1e-8 / 1e-7 relative on the fixture, `Cor_1` to 1e-9 |
| D22 | Student-t nu prior | any prior on `nu` (`real<lower=1> nu`), renormalised on `nu > 1` (`normal(5, 2)`, `student_t(3, 0, 10)`, `cauchy(0, 5)`, `exponential(0.1)`, ...), and `gamma(2, 0.1)` by default | `Priors(nu=...)` is a `Gamma`, `Exponential`, `HalfNormal(s)` (brms `normal(0, s)`) or `HalfCauchy(s)` (brms `cauchy(0, s)`) prior, truncated to `nu > 1` and renormalised; a location prior and `HalfStudentT` are refused (as is `InverseGamma`, A07), and no default is filled in (D1; `ab.default_priors()` returns brms's `gamma(2, 0.1)` on request, A07) | kept (A13): PyMC renormalises a truncation through the prior's CDF, which it has for these four and not for `HalfStudentT`; the fixture `student_learned_nu` uses `gamma(2, 0.1)` |
| D23 | default priors of a fixed Student-t nu | `default_prior()` of a `student()` model always lists `nu` with `gamma(2, 0.1)`: a fixed nu is a `constant(4)` prior given afterwards | `ab.default_priors(..., family="student", nu=4)` has no `nu` prior (the model has no nu parameter); with `nu=None`, the default, it is brms's `gamma(2, 0.1)` | documented (A07): G6 compares the fixture `student_varint` (nu fixed at 4) under `nu=None`, the model brms's `default_prior()` describes |
| D24 | censoring | `y \| cens(c)`: a row coded -1 adds `normal_lcdf(y \| mu, sigma)`, 1 adds `normal_lccdf`, 0 the density; Stan's `normal_lcdf` differentiates through piecewise polynomial approximations (Abramowitz and Stegun 7.1.26, Taylor fits), off by up to 4e-4 absolute; brms also reads the codes `"left"`/`"none"`/`"right"`, `TRUE`/`FALSE` and interval censoring (2 with `cens(c, y2)`), and combines `cens()` with `trunc()` | `cens()` is offered for the Gaussian family (P6), with an exact log-CDF on both paths; codes other than -1, 0, 1, `y2`, `cens()` with `trunc()` and families other than the Gaussian are refused | accepted against two references (P6; parked in B02): the frozen fixture `gauss_censored` records brms gradients off the exact (50-digit) ones by 2.3e-7, 2.5e-6, 2.1e-6, 2.1e-7 and 3.4e-7 relative at points 0, 4, 5, 6 and 7 (GRAD_REL 1e-7; at point 4 the gradient in `sd_1` is 3.4398114909533386 where the exact value is 3.4398200705418716); the case opts in for its gradient, and 32 of its 160 gradient entries, at those five points, pass on the committed 50-digit reference, which AutoBayes matches to 8.7e-15 at every entry; every log density (brms within 2e-16 of the reference) and every other gate agrees with brms (docs/addition-terms.md) |
| D25 | truncation | `y \| trunc(lb, ub)` with numbers or per-row variables: each row's density minus `normal_lccdf(lb \| ...)`, `normal_lcdf(ub \| ...)` or their `log_diff_exp`, with `normal_lccdf` formed as `1 - erf` by subtraction; `posterior_epred` is the truncated mean formed with `pnorm(ub) - pnorm(lb)` by subtraction; a censored row of a truncated model is the untruncated tail minus the truncation mass | `trunc()` is offered for the Gaussian family (P6; exact log mass, truncated mean and draws); numeric bounds only, `trunc()` without a finite bound, `cens()` with `trunc()` and families other than the Gaussian are refused | accepted against two references (P6; parked in B02): at point 4 of the frozen fixture `gauss_truncated`, where a row's location sits 6.17 SD below the bound, brms's gradient in `b[1]` is -1.5193463027823753 where the exact value is -1.5193469340177965 (4.2e-7 relative, GRAD_REL 1e-7) and its `posterior_epred` is off by up to 1.05e-3 relative (PRED_REL 1e-9) at 6 of 656 entries; the case opts in for its gradient and expected values, and 2 of its 160 gradient entries (`b[1]` at point 4, with and without the Jacobian) and those 6 expected values pass on the committed 50-digit reference, which AutoBayes matches to 3.7e-14 (gradient) and 8.3e-15 (expected values); every log density (brms within 6.7e-11 of the reference) and every other gate agrees with brms (docs/addition-terms.md) |
| D26 | weights | `weights(w)` accepts zero weights, and `weights(w, scale = TRUE)` rescales them to sum to the number of rows | the weights must be positive and finite; `scale = TRUE` is refused with the hint to rescale the column | kept (B02): a zero-weight row adds nothing to the likelihood but still enters the centring means and the level sets, so it is dropped instead; positive weights are verified against brms on one frozen case per family (`gauss_weighted`, `<family>_weighted`) |
